How long does delivery take?

Plastic anatomical models usually arrive in 2 days. 3D-printed titanium implants take 5 days, because they need specialised printing, quality control and regulatory checks. Complex designs or your location can change this.

How much does a printed bone model cost?

It depends on the size and complexity of the model, the material and printing process, the quantity, and post-processing and shipping. Send the scans and we’ll quote.

Is my patient’s data safe?

Scans are de-identified before our engineers open them, and each case is shared only with the people you invite.

Which files can I send?

DICOM series from CT or MRI, or existing STL models.
